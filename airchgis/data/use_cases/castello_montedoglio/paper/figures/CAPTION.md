Terrain visualization of Castello di Montedoglio, Sansepolcro, Italy. Left: elevation; centre and right: RVT hillshade with illumination azimuths 315° and 45°, respectively, both at a solar altitude of 35° and vertical factor 1. White areas indicate missing survey data. Coordinates are offsets from the south-west corner of the metric raster. Geographic inputs were reprojected using bilinear interpolation; the supplied source DTM was not modified. These panels illustrate the effect of illumination direction and do not establish archaeological interpretations. See [processing metadata](../terrain_metadata.json) and [source provenance](../../provenance.json) for settings and attribution.
