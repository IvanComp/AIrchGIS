\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item AIrch unifies LiDAR, Sentinel-2/Landsat and 3D views in one no-code workbench
\item Detection scores are shown as priorities, with their sources and uncertainty
\item A built-in benchmark measures every layer against known sites and chance
\item Field-boundary contamination of candidates falls from 50\% to 5\%
\item Sentinel-2 cannot resolve 5\,m Roman streets: single-date SNR is only 1.6
\end{itemize}
