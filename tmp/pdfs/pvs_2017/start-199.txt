Piomeco, Colle Magno 43,166827°, 12.913596° 0.084 ba (40x21 m)

Interpretation Late Bronze and/or Early Iron Age settlement (farm) (ype 9) (13*-7" century BC); important Roman and
Medieval off-site activity.

Topography Situated at the congruence of two brooks near the Colle Magno, on the location of an ancient water source
in the form of a semi-circular depression.

Investigation = Surveyed in 2000 in the upper Potenza valley. Concentration of impasto pottery, flint and animal bones
from recently ploughed up archacological contexts (possibly pits) in association with dense arcas of
pebbles. Irregular anomalies confirmed by aerial photography.

Artefacts Pre-/protohistoric pottery, animal bone (pig, cow, sheep, goat; unburned), rolled limestone pebbles and
sandstones, Artefact density: 4174.3/ha (001), 8.8/ha (off-site).

Pioraco, Monte Primo 43.164193°, 12. 994884" 20 ha (160x130 m)

Interpretation —_ Lange Bronze and Iron Age periodical nucleated settlement and aggregation site for special activities
(feasting, cattle mustering cic.) and communal sanctuary (type /0).

Topography The hilltop site im the upper Potenza valley is protected by four concentric carthworks (average height 2
m). These possible remains of artificial walls (and ditches?) around the top of the Monte Primo (1299 m)
are linked to a small limestone quarry and a series of wider extemal enclosures and former land divisions.

Investigation Known from literature; randomly explored in 2000, 2001 and 2005 and registered through aerial
photography (2000-2005), with the latter technique revealing the internal organisation of the site

Artefacts Pre-/protohistoric pottery, animal bone (sheep, goat, pig; bummed and unburned). Artefact density: 17.2/ha
