Camerino, Campo di Concentramento 43,195475°, 13,051126° 0.78 ha (130x60 m)

Interpretation Rural settlement (large) villa) (ne 5).

Topography Situated on the steep first terrace south of the valley bottom, in the upper Potenza valley area.

Investigation Identified through aerial photography in the spring of 2002. The site was characterized by crop marks
indicating the presence of a large rectangular and internally divided stone building (min. 20x10 m) in its
eastern part and a whole series of secondary structures (possibly buildings, cisterns, pits, ditches, ...) west
of it, This division probably reflects a rural villa with separated residential and productive areas, The
aenal survey was followed by individual examimation in the field in 2003, while a revisit in 2015 fully
confirmed the finds.

Artefacts Large and quite dense concentration of Roman Imperial pottery (c.g. amphorac (Dressel 2-4)) and
building material (¢.g. many tiles, bricks, floor slabs, lumps of concrete). Artefact density: 1300/ha (100);
445Wha (2015).
