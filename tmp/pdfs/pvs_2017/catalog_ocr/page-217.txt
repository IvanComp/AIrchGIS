Pioraco, Fonte Venere 43.170527°, 13.034524° 0.15 ha (60x25 m)

Interpretation — Rural settlement (medium-sized farm) (type 3).

Topography Situated on the EW oriented slope near the stream Rio Selvazzano, in the southern part of the upper
Potenza valley. Two minor concentrations were visible (more or less in situ and not the result of material
coming down from the slope).

Investigation Identified through systematic survey in 2000, followed by individual examination and then confirmed by
acrial photography.

pebbles, Roman tegulae) sometimes articulated in zones of somewhat denser scatters. The site also
comains a good number of (off-site) Medieval pottery sherds. Artefact density: 846.7/ha (060); 22.3/ha
