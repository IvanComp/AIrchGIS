Interpretation Roman urban settlement, probably preceded by smaller [ron Age settlement, and terminated as an Early

Topography The site of the former town of Porentia, near the Adriatic coast, is connected with the now

Investigation First located by N. Alficri during the late 1940s and via rescuc-excavations of a northem housing sector

magnetic surveys and partial coverage with GPR and clectric resistivity prospections, geomorphological
coring operations and total station measurements of the microtopography, excavations of the W-gate

2003). The main results of all this work are discussed elsewhere in the book.
