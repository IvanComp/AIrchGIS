Topography The artefact scatter is located on the flat first terrace of the Potenza, immediately north of the river
Potenza and at the foot of the N-S oriented slope. The site continues to the west, where it is disturbed by
modem constructions.

Investigation Site identified through systematic survey in 2001, followed by individual examination and then confirmed
by aerial photography (2001-2005), which shows light-coloured soil marks of the croded gravel terrace,
the light-coloured linear trace of the Roman road continuing in the field towards the west, and two distinct
areas of greyish soil coinciding with artefact scatters. A rescuc excavation in 2007 (with systematic trail
trenches covering 10% of the terrain), coordinated by the Soprintendenza per i Beni Archeologici delle
Marche, demonstrated that these two greyish zones coincided exactly with the locations of two Roman
houses (dated to 1.3" century AD). Of the rectangular houses remained only a series of mostly spoliated
wall structures, made of roughly cut sandstone and some large river pebbles, and a few fragments of brick
floors.

Artefacts The surface scatter contained Roman building materials (roof tiles, lange sandstone blocks), a wide array
of pottery (especially jugs) and other Roman artefacts, A series of flint artefacts demonstrate the earlier
Stone Age activity on these riverside terraces. Artefact density: 639.7/ha (079), 17_2/ha (off-site).
