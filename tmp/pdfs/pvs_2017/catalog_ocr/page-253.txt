Treia, Contrada S$. Carlo 2 43.31708°, 13.274852° \

Interpretation Roman aqueduct (type 8).

Topography Located at a distance of 1100 m from the western wall of Roman Trea, in a field to the north of S.P. N.
169 road.

Investigation Re-survey of a site found by Moscatelli; Umberto Moscatelli had already put forward the theory that the
course of the Roman aqueduct lay to the west of Jrea running parallel to the S.P.N. 169. In 2007, we
were able to confirm his findings.

Artefacts Several large fragments of terracotta hydraulic pipes were discovered. Although we were able to survey
the fields to the west of this location, no other traces of the aqueduct were observed.
