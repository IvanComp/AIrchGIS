Topography Two large artefact scatters are located on the flat first terrace of the river Potenza, immediately north of

Investigation Site identified through systematic survey m 2001, followed by individual examination and then confirmed
by acrial photography (2001-2005). The aerial view shows light-coloured soil marks (ca. 10m wide) of
two possible gravel roads and their junction: the valley road with a NE-SW orientation and road of street
with a NW-SE orientation, of local significance or possibly linked to the Roman town of Trea. The aerial
views also showed several distinet arcas of greyish soil coinciding with artefact scatters. A rescuc
excavation in 2007 (with systematic trial trenches covering 10% of the terrain), coordinated by the
Soprintendenza per i Beni Archeologici delle Marche, demonstrated that two greyish zones reflected
exactly the presence of remains of Roman houses (dated to the 1"-3" century AD), while a series of
simple Roman burials (some covered with tiles) were found in between these areas. Of the rectangular
houses remained only a series of mostly spoliated wall structures, made or roughly cut sandstone and
some large river pebbles.
