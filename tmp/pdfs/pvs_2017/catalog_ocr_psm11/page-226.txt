Interpretation

Large settlement — central and western part of a village-type of Roman settlement (see also 079) — and

Topography

Two large artefact scatters are located on the flat first terrace of the river Potenza, immediately north of

the stream and at the foot of the N-S oriented slope. The large site continues to the cast, where it is

disturbed by modern constructions,

Investigation

Site identified through systematic survey m 2001, followed by individual examination and then confirmed

by acrial photography (2001-2005). The aerial view shows light-coloured soil marks (ca. 10m wide) of

two possible gravel roads and their junction: the valley road with a NE-SW orientation and road of street

with a NW-SE orientation, of local significance or possibly linked to the Roman town of Trea. The aerial

views also showed several distinct areas of greyish soil coinciding with artefact scatters. A rescuc

excavation in 2007 (with systematic trial trenches covering 10% of the terrain), coordinated by the

Soprintendenza per i Beni Archcologici delle Marche, demonstrated that two greyish zones reflected

exactly the presenee of remains of Roman houses (dated to the 1*.3% century AD), while a series of

simple Roman burials (some covered with tiles) were found in between these areas. Of the rectangular

houses remained only a series of mostly spoliated wall structures, made or roughly cut sandstone and

some large river pebbles.

Artefacts

Broad range of ceramics (¢.g. BG, TS (Conspectus 34, 2xConspectus 18/21), TW, RA (c.g. Dressel 2-4,

LRA), Firma lamps, ARS (Hayes 50, 50A, SOB, 60, 67, ACW (2xLRCW 4, LRCW 6) and a wide varicty

of Roman building material (tegulae, limestone and sandstone blocks, mortar, bricks of opus spicatum

floors, ete.). Artefact density: 1065.8/ha (094).
