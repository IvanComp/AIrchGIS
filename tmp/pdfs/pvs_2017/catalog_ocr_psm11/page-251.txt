Interpretation

Centralized settlement (look-our?).

Topography

Imposing ca. 0 m-high hilltop site in the central segment of the Potenza valley, north of the river. The

impressive location visually controls the valley from the Apennines to the Adriatic sea.

Investigation

Punctual excavations near the summit by Lollini showed the presence of a large settlement which acted as

a central place between the seventh and the third centuries BC. It is connected with the important Picene

cemetery directly NE of the hilltop at Monte Penna, Individual survey (M. Sterry) in 2007, followed by

periodic field checks and drone mapping (2013), provided indications for ancient enclosure systems in the

less steep western part, while a strong presence of Iron Age roof tiles, impasto pottery and also some

Roman Republican sherds was noticed here and there.

Artefacts

Mostly Iron Age impasto ware (c.g. dolia, fine and coarse ware) and protohistoric building material, with

some Roman Imperial materia
