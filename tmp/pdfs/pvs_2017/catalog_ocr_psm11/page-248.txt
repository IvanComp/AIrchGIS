Anterpretation Roman urban settlement, probably preceded by a Roman Republican village (type 7).

Topography

The ancient town site of Ricina is located on a flat river terrace directly north of the river Potenza, at the

beginning of its lower valley course, some 15 km from the coast and a few kilometres north of Macerata.

The site is today taken in by some arable land, but also by the small present-day agglomeration of Villa

Potenza. Only remains of a theatre, a cistern and some isolated fragments of building walls are visible

above ground today.

lovestigation

Rescue excavations by the Soprintendenza and other actors during the 20" century revealed parts of a

Roman street lined with shops and elements of several domus, A more systematic topographical survey

approach with extensive mapping of many new elements (houses, shops, amphitheatre, temple, ...) was

started by the PVS team in 2004 and the main field oriented operations during the past decade were:

detection and continuous monitoring with oblique and low altitude acrial photography, geophysical

prospections (GPR and magnetic survey) of a large northem/central part of the site (2013), limited

geomorphological coring operations, and a gridded artefact survey of a large northern/central part of the

site (2005), The maim results of all this work are discussed elsewhere in the book. Ilere we present only the

finds from the grid survey.
