Investigation

Partly known from literature, located through acrial photography (2003 May); focused artefact survey in

2003 and regular controls of ploughed up building materials; re-surveyed with line survey in 2012; a

partial magnetometer and earth resistance survey in 2009 revealed only few traces; full coverage

magnetometer survey in 2010 and GPR survey in 2012 allowed for the mapping of building structures and

other features. Recent additions of structural evidence were achieved by the use of other remote sensing

material (c.g. satellite data) and field control of the northem edge of the site.

Artefacts

others were probably destroyed during roadworks, A connected main artefact concentration (A) consists

mostly of Roman amphora sherds, some of them overfired (30-50 sherds/m*), while also an oven wall

fragment was found here. The second concentration (B) in the southem part of the field coincides with the
